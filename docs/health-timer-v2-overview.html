Daily lifecycle

One cycle of the timer, from countdown to logged completion.

  1. Step 1 · Countdown

    TimerService ticks every second; timer:tick pushes seconds to the widget, shown as MM:SS with a hairline progress rule.

  2. Step 2 · Break triggers

    Threshold reached → a random task fires via task:triggered + a desktop notification. The TaskCard appears.

  3. Step 3 · Complete or snooze

    Hoàn thành logs history + stats and plays a celebration; or snooze 5 / 10 / 15 min, which lowers the threshold and auto-restores the original cycle after firing.

  4. Step 4 · Cycle resumes

    Timer resets and the countdown restarts. The Insights dashboard tracks streak, total completed, and recent history.

Three break types cycle randomly: hydration, movement (2 min), eye rest (20-20-20 rule).

Architecture & data flow

Main process owns the timer, notifications, and storage; the renderer talks to it only through the preload contextBridge API.

Notes

Data storage & defaults

Settings, history, and stats persist via electron-store at %APPDATA%\health-timer\config\config.json. Default interval 60 min, theme "system", history capped at 100 records.

Known limitations

Windows-only. Single timer instance. The setInterval timer may drift slightly over very long uptime; a >= threshold check ensures skipped ticks still fire on the next caught tick.
